Common Phishing Vectors

Phishing is the fraudulent attempt to obtain sensitive information or deploy malicious payloads by impersonating a trustworthy entity.

📧
Mass Distribution

Standard Email Phishing

Mass-distributed deceptive emails disguised as notifications from well-known institutions (banks, shipping providers, cloud vendors) aimed at harvesting login credentials or banking data.

🎯
Targeted Attack

Spear Phishing

Tailored and customized attacks aimed at a specific individual or organization. Attackers leverage OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) from LinkedIn and company directories to appear authentic.

👑
High-Value Target

Whaling (Executive Fraud)

Phishing attempts targeting C-level executives, finance managers, or VIPs to initiate fraudulent wire transfers (Business Email Compromise - BEC) or expose confidential corporate records.

📱
Mobile / SMS

Smishing (SMS Phishing)

Deceptive SMS messages urging users to click malicious shortened links (e.g., fake package delivery tracking, bank fraud warnings, or tax refund alerts).

📞
Voice Phishing

Vishing

Fraudulent phone calls or automated voice systems impersonating bank fraud departments or tech support to manipulate victims into revealing 2FA codes or granting remote device access.

📑
Replica Attack

Clone Phishing

Attackers clone a legitimate email previously received by the victim, substituting authentic attachments or links with malicious ones and claiming it to be an "updated version".

Anatomy of a Phishing Email

Inspect this simulated attack email. Highlighted boxes reveal the primary red flags cyber analysts check for.

From: IT Support <helpdesk@micros0ft-security-auth.net>
To: employee@company.com
Subject: [URGENT] Your Password Expires in 2 Hours!

Dear Valued User,


We detected unauthorized login attempts from an unknown device in Moscow, Russia. Your enterprise account has been flagged for immediate suspension.


To retain access to your mailbox and shared corporate files, you must verify your identity and update your password immediately within the next 120 minutes.


Update My Password Now
⚠️ Hovering reveals actual destination: http://192.168.1.100/login.php?ref=steal

Failure to respond will lead to permanent account termination.


Best Regards,
Microsoft Global Security Center

🚨 1. Sender Spoofing & Lookalikes

Notice the domain micros0ft-security-auth.net uses the number '0' instead of 'o' (Typosquatting) and is completely unaffiliated with Microsoft's official domain.

🚨 2. Artificial Sense of Urgency

Phrases like "Expires in 2 Hours" or "Account Terminated" induce panic, short-circuiting critical thinking and rushing the victim into complying.

🚨 3. Mismatched Hyperlinks

Always inspect hyperlinks prior to clicking. The button displays a legitimate action, but directs to an arbitrary IP address or malicious credential harvester.

Spotting Deceptive & Fake Websites

Attackers build pixel-perfect clones of login portals. Here is how to distinguish clones from authentic domains.

🔍

Typosquatting & Combosquatting

Attackers register domain names intentionally misspelled or appended with keywords:

• paypal-verification-center.com (Fake)
• netfIix.com (Using capital 'I' for lowercase 'l')
• apple-support-id88.net (Fake)

🔒

The "HTTPS = Safe" Fallacy

A padlock icon / HTTPS only indicates that transmission is encrypted; it does not guarantee the destination is legitimate. Over 80% of phishing sites today use free SSL certificates (Let's Encrypt).

🌐

Punycode (Homograph Attacks)

Attackers exploit internationalized domain names (IDN) with lookalike Cyrillic or Greek characters. A character like Cyrillic а looks identical to Latin a, but translates to xn--... in browser address bars.

Defense Strategy & Best Practices

Implement these defensive measures across personal and corporate environments to neutralize social engineering threats.

✓

Enforce Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA / 2FA)

Always enforce FIDO2 / WebAuthn hardware keys or authenticator apps (TOTP). Avoid SMS-based 2FA where possible due to SIM swapping and SS7 intercept risks.

✓

Implement Email Authentication Protocols (SPF, DKIM, DMARC)

Organizations should configure DMARC p=reject policies alongside SPF and cryptographic DKIM records to prevent unauthorized servers from spoofing company domains.

✓

Bookmark Official Portals & Type URLs Directly

Never navigate to sensitive banking, email, or cloud infrastructure logins directly from email links. Always use browser bookmarks or type verified URLs manually.

✓

Out-of-Band Verification for Financial & Sensitive Requests

If a manager or vendor requests an emergency invoice payment or wire transfer change, verify via a secondary, known phone number before proceeding.
